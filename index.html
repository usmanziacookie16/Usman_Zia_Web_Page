About

Between 2016 and 2019 I worked as a professional game artist in Lahore, specializing in hard-surface modeling of military vehicles and aircraft — the kind of work where a rivet out of place or a panel line that doesn't read at a glance gets noticed.

I'm now completing an M.Sc. in Computational Social Systems (Human-Technology Interaction) at RWTH Aachen, where my research looks at how anthropomorphic cues in voice and avatar agents shape trust and reflection. Alongside the 3D work, I build the real-time systems that make conversational AI actually feel responsive — Three.js front-ends, WebSocket backends, and voice agents running on the OpenAI Realtime API.
